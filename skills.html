ENGINEERING PERSPECTIVE

How I Think About Building Software

Core engineering observations that guide my architectural choices and code quality.

OBSERVATION 01

Choose the Simplest Architecture

Over-engineering solves imaginary problems at the expense of real timeline and budget. Choose the simplest architecture that solves the actual business requirement cleanly.

OBSERVATION 02

Separate Business Logic from UI

Keeping controllers and backend business logic independent of user presentation allows services to be tested thoroughly and adapted without rewriting core rules.

OBSERVATION 03

Maintainability Before Premature Optimization

Readable code with predictable variable names, clear directory structures, and type validations will save tenfold more engineering hours than esoteric micro-optimizations.

OBSERVATION 04

Use AI Where It Creates Real Value

Integrate AI models and LLMs where they measurably eliminate repetitive manual workflows, parse complex unstructured text, or power helpful search—not simply for marketing badges.
